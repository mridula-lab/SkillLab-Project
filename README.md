# SkillLab-Project
### **Problem Statement:**
Users often struggle to identify phishing, scam, and fake emails. Suspicious links, unknown senders, and misleading content can make emails difficult to trust. Important emails may also be wrongly moved to the spam folder, while repeated unwanted emails may not be properly flagged. This makes it difficult for users to identify threats and manage their inbox effectively.
### **Requirements:**
##### The system should:
- Allow users to view or upload an email for analysis.
- Categorize emails as Safe, Spam, Suspicious, or Potentially Harmful.
- Help users identify phishing and scam emails.
- Highlight suspicious links, unknown senders, and misleading content.
- Identify repeated or similar unwanted emails.
- Help users find important emails that may have been wrongly moved to spam.
- Clearly show why an email has been flagged.
- Display a simple risk level or threat score for each email.
- Allow users to take quick actions on flagged emails.
- Present the information through a simple, clear, and user-friendly interface.

### Possible Solutions
- Use AI-based email analysis to categorize emails based on their content and characteristics.
- Display sender and domain information to help users identify unfamiliar or suspicious senders.
- Analyse links within emails and show a simple safe/risky indicator.
- Highlight phishing or scam-related patterns in the email content.
- Group similar emails and alert users about repeated spam.
- Provide an Important Emails section to help users review emails that may have been wrongly marked as spam.
- Display a simple Low, Medium, or High risk level for each email.
- Show easy-to-understand explanations such as “Suspicious link detected” or “Unknown sender”.
- Provide quick actions such as Move to Inbox, Report Spam, Block Sender, or Mark as Important.
- Provide a dashboard showing recent threats, spam activity, and important alerts.
